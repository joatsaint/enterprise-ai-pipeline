# Free Rent Tracker for Small Landlords — Setup Guide

This is a free tool that uses Google's AI (Gemini) to build and keep a rent
log for you, using the payment emails you already get from Zelle, Cash App,
Venmo, or your bank. You don't need to know anything about AI to use it —
just follow the steps below.

## What you need first

- A Google account (free — if you don't have one, go to
  [accounts.google.com](https://accounts.google.com) and click "Create
  account").
- Your rent payment emails need to land in a Gmail inbox. If they currently
  go somewhere else (Hotmail, Yahoo, etc.), that's fine — the tool will tell
  you how to forward them during setup.

## Setup steps

1. **Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)** and sign in with
   your Google account.
2. **Open `SETUP_PROMPT.md`** (in this same folder), copy everything below
   the line, and paste it into the Gemini chat box.
3. **Answer Gemini's questions as they come**, one at a time — your
   properties, your Gmail address, and so on. There's no wrong answer here,
   just tell it what's true for your situation.
4. When Gemini creates your spreadsheet, it will land in your Google Drive.
   **If it doesn't show up in the folder you wanted, that's normal** — just
   open Google Drive, find the file (it's usually the most recent one), and
   drag it into whatever folder you like.
5. From then on, whenever you want to log new payments, just open that same
   Gemini conversation (or start a new one and re-paste the prompt) and say:
   **"Check for new payments."**

## A few honest notes

- This is a DIY tool, not a paid service. You own the spreadsheet completely
  — nothing is stored anywhere except your own Google Drive.
- **Your data stays in your own Google account.** I (Randy) never see your
  emails, your spreadsheet, or your tenants' information — Gemini is working
  inside your account only, the same as if you'd typed the formulas in
  yourself.
- It will never guess a dollar amount. If it's not sure a payment matches
  the right property, it'll ask you instead of assuming.
- This isn't legal, tax, or financial advice — I'm not an attorney or an
  accountant. It's just a way to stop manually typing in rent payments every
  month.
