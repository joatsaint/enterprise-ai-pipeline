# Guide Positioning

A structural technique, not a tone. This is HOW a creator earns the right to
pitch, distilled from tearing down a real, currently-running competitor
funnel (see `docs/Brain Droppings/Symoné B. Tech -Claude Just Changed GovTech
Forever.txt` for the full source transcript and
`memory/reference_symone_btech_guide_positioning_teardown.md` for the full
analysis). Ties directly to `knowledge/brand/brandscript.md`'s existing
Guide/Hero framing — this skill is the execution-level move for stage 3
("AND MEETS A GUIDE") of that BrandScript, not a competing framework.

**Toolkit, not a formula** — same standing rule as every other style skill
in this project (`infomercial-pitch`, the war-story recipes). Use the stages
that fit; skip what doesn't; never force all 8 into a piece that doesn't
need them.

## The 8 stages

1. **Inflate the problem before naming any solution.** State the stakes/scale
   first — real numbers, real named entities, real urgency — with zero pitch
   attached yet. The reader has to feel the size of the gap before "I can
   help" means anything.

2. **Frame the gap as structural, not personal.** The reader isn't behind
   because they're inadequate — the *system* changed and hasn't caught up.
   This lets you introduce yourself as the bridge without the opening move
   being an insult.

3. **Voice the reader's exact objection in first person, before they can
   raise it.** The core move. Literally write the sentence the skeptical
   reader is thinking — "I don't even have the basics yet" / "I've heard
   this AI-hype pitch before" — then answer it in the same breath. This
   turns the objection into the transition into your point, not a reason to
   leave. Never skip this stage when writing a closing/CTA section; it's the
   single highest-leverage move in the whole pattern.

4. **Build authority through specificity, not credentials.** Don't claim
   "I'm an expert." Name real things — real tools, real numbers, real
   sources, a real war story with a date attached. Specificity substitutes
   for a resume and is instantly checkable, which fits this project's
   Citation Guard discipline better than a credentials claim would anyway.

5. **Give a real, honest roadmap — simple enough to trust, incomplete enough
   to imply the reader still benefits from the guide.** Don't gatekeep the
   actual information. Give the real steps. The natural gap between "what to
   do" and "how to do it under real constraints, fast" is what earns the
   CTA — it isn't manufactured by withholding.

6. **Escalating commitment ladder, cheapest ask first.** Free/low-friction
   asset → deeper free asset → the actual CTA. Matches the existing Riddle
   of Steel funnel shape (free 14-day plan → paid guide) — this stage
   doesn't require new infrastructure, just sequencing what already exists.

   **Timing rule within this stage (confirmed via Mark Savant footage,
   2026-08-11):** never pitch the paid thing until the content itself has
   already made the viewer feel the specific problem it solves. The free
   ask goes early — right after the hook, framed as reciprocity ("this
   helps me make better content for you"), zero friction, easy yes. The
   paid ask goes last, only after the piece has spent real time proving
   the pain point it resolves — it's earned by the content, not inserted
   on a schedule. Placing the paid ask early (before the pain is felt)
   reads as a hard sell regardless of how well stage 3 is executed.

7. **Contrarian redirection reinforces the guide framing.** Name the thing
   most readers default to, dismiss it plainly, redirect to the real path.
   Only use this with something genuinely true — never invent a strawman
   just to look contrarian; that fails Citation Guard's Technical Claims
   check the same as any other unverified assertion.

8. **One concrete numeric anchor as the emotional payoff.** A specific
   number the reader can repeat back to themselves, not a vague
   improvement. Must be real/sourced or explicitly framed as Randy's own
   lived estimate, per the existing Statistics and Numbers rule.

## Where this plugs into existing skills

- **Articles** (`create-next-article`): apply stage 3 in the closing
  section, right before the Standing CTA line — this is a sharper move than
  what's currently there. Doesn't replace the CTA format rule, it's what
  precedes it.
- **Shorts/scripts** (`shorts-script`, `youtube-script`): stage 3 fits the
  REFRAME beat; stages 1-2 fit HOOK/SETUP; stage 6 governs which CTA to use
  depending on what's already been offered earlier in a series.
- **title-generator**: stage 8's numeric anchor is exactly what Formula 2
  (The Confession) and Formula 5 (The Specificity Hook) are built to
  surface — pull the anchor number from here when generating those.

## Hard boundaries — do not import from the source material

- **No artificial scarcity/exclusivity gating** ("we don't work with
  everyone," application-only access). Randy's offer is a free guide and a
  real paid product, not a screened high-ticket program — this move doesn't
  fit and reads as a mismatch if copied literally.
- **No inflated/unverifiable claims** used purely for shock value (the
  source material's "AI model as strong as a nuke" framing). Every
  specificity claim (stage 4) and numeric anchor (stage 8) still goes
  through Citation Guard exactly as normal — this skill changes structure,
  not the truth bar.
- **No multi-tier paid-application funnel.** Stage 6's ladder tops out at
  the existing Riddle of Steel CTA — don't invent new commitment rungs to
  mimic the source's structure for its own sake.

## Gotchas

- Stage 3 is easy to write flat ("some of you might be thinking...") —
  the source material's power comes from writing the objection as a real,
  specific first-person sentence, not a paraphrase. Write it the way the
  reader would actually think it, contractions and all.
- Don't run all 8 stages in a single Short (60-75s) — stages 1, 3, and 8
  are the highest-value three for that length; stages 2, 4, 5, 7 need more
  runway and fit articles or longer-form video better.
